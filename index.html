How it works

From idea to finished book.

A smart, step-by-step process with two moments where you stay in control.

01
You share your idea

A few words or a detailed description — whatever you have.

02
AI builds the plan

Titles, characters, plot arcs — the full blueprint for your series.

03
Review & safety check

The plan is checked for quality, tone, and appropriateness.

✓

05

✓

One final review before your book is packaged and exported.

06
Your book is ready

Delivered as a clean document, ready to read, print, or publish.
